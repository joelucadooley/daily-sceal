Daily Scéal

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Daily Scéal is a free project for learning Irish through the news, made by Joe Luca Dooley. It comes as a website (dailysceal.com) and an iOS app. This page covers both.

The iOS app

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Everything the app keeps stays on your phone: the stories it downloads for offline reading, the words you save to Mo Chúpla Focal, your flashcard progress, your chosen reading level and whether you've turned on the daily reminder. If you delete the app, all of that is deleted with it.

The app connects to dailysceal.com to download the day's stories and the verified word list. Those are plain files; the request contains nothing about you beyond what any web request contains, and we don't log it.

The optional 12:00 reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. No notification service or push token is involved, and it can be switched off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Tapping "Read the full story on RTÉ" opens rte.ie in Safari, where RTÉ's privacy policy applies.

The website

dailysceal.com uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not store personal information. Your reading level and any saved preferences are kept in your own browser only.

Children

Daily Scéal is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
