Common questions

A translation looks wrong.

Tell me and it gets fixed for good. Most words come from a hand-checked dictionary, and the rest are machine translated so the stories can go out every morning. Flashcards and gap fill only ever use the hand-checked words.

How does a word become learned?

Every word you save to Mo Chúpla Focal has four dots. Get it right in Flashcards and a dot fills in, one a day at most. Fill all four and the word is learned, and it stays learned. Miss it and it comes back the next day.

Where do the stories come from?

Headlines and short summaries are from RTÉ News. Daily Scéal adds the Irish and links to the full story on rte.ie.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is saved on your phone. See the privacy policy.

The stories didn't update today.

New stories usually land late morning. Pull down on the list of stories to refresh. If a day is missing, the daily generator may have hit a snag; it usually sorts itself out by the next morning.

Can I hear the words spoken?

Not yet in the app. It's on the list.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and has no accounts. The full privacy policy covers both the app and the website.
