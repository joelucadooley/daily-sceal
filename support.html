Common questions

Why does a word say "not yet verified"?
Words marked verified come from a hand-checked dictionary. The rest are machine translated so that stories can go out every day, and they're labelled so you know to treat them with care. Flashcards and gap fill only ever use verified words. If you spot a wrong one, tell me and it gets fixed permanently.

Where do the stories come from?
Headlines and short summaries are from RTÉ News. Daily Scéal adds the Irish and links to the full story on rte.ie.

Do I need an account?
No. Everything is saved on your phone. See the privacy policy.

The stories didn't update today.
New stories usually land late morning. Pull down on the Stories screen to refresh. If a day is missing, the daily generator may have hit a snag; it usually sorts itself by the next morning.

Can I hear the words spoken?
Not yet in the app. It's on the list.
